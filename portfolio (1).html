7. Key Findings & Recommendations
// business-actionable insights derived from the pipeline
In [9]:
print("=== PIPELINE DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY ===")
Finding 1 — SLA Breach
26.9% of all 1,436 shipments breached SLA (386 late orders). The worst single delay was +20.2 days on ORD_01002 via FedEx Trans, generating a risk score of 1.29 — the highest in the dataset.
Finding 2 — Carrier Flag: DHL Express
DHL Express has the highest SLA breach rate at 31.4% (116/369 orders) and the highest avg risk score at 0.286. Recommend SLA renegotiation or volume reallocation to Delhivery.
Finding 3 — Best Performer: Delhivery
Delhivery has the lowest late rate (22.8%), lowest risk score (0.231), and best avg lead time (−1.53 days ahead of SLA). Recommended as priority carrier for high-value shipments.
Finding 4 — Critical Risk Band
404 orders (28.1%) fall in the Critical risk band (score ≥ 0.50). All top-10 highest risk orders are confirmed late with freight costs near maximum (₹1,700–1,800), indicating a strong correlation between freight overhead and delivery failure.
Finding 5 — Value Risk Score Utility
The engineered KPI (60% delay weight + 40% freight ratio) successfully isolates late high-cost shipments. FedEx Trans dominates the top-10 risk list (6/10 orders), confirming systematic failure for premium-priced shipments.
